About Joey's Farm

Joey's Farm is an independent, family-run business based in the Philippines. We raise pasture-fed hens and are committed to sustainable, ethical egg farming.

We simply want to produce the freshest, highest-quality eggs and provide dependable service. When you order from us, you're supporting a farm that genuinely cares about its hens, its customers, and its community.

We're proud to be small, sustainable, and rooted in the community we serve. We're in this for the long haul, and every order matters to us.

— The Joey's Farm Team
